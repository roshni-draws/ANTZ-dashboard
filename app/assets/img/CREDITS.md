# Image credits

All photos come from Wikimedia Commons and are bundled locally (no hotlinking). Each was
resized to 800px wide and re-encoded as JPG (quality 70); the app crops them with `object-fit: cover`.
Total size is about 1.6 MB.

**Attribution in the app:** Profile menu (avatar, top right) > **Photo credits** lists every photo with
author, source link and licence link. The list comes from `config.js` > `credits`; keep it in sync
when you add or swap a photo. CC BY-SA photos remain under their CC BY-SA licence.

No people appear in any photo. The header avatar has no photo and shows the initials "RS".
The planned `keeper.jpg` was swapped for `wild-dog-pack.jpg` to avoid photographing people.

## Add or replace a photo
1. Use only CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA, public domain or Unsplash-licensed images, and avoid identifiable people.
2. Resize to about 800px wide JPG and copy it here.
3. Map it in `config.js` > `images`, and add a row to `config.js` > `credits` and to the table above.
